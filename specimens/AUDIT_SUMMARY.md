## How defects are scored

A finding matches a planted defect when it is reported for the defect's file and names the defect's
function. `detected` means the finding text also carries a keyword for the defect's category;
`function_flagged` means the audit flagged that function for something else; `missed` means nothing.
"Target" categories are the ones the audit is designed to check today (arithmetic, bounds, null,
preconditions, invariants, state transitions, and the Solidity reentrancy/access-control/unchecked-call
heuristics). Findings that match no planted defect are listed per specimen in `audit/coverage.json`
under `unmatched_findings`; they are either extra real issues or false positives and need human review.

## How findings are scored

Each finding (counterexample entries excluded, they support another finding) gets one status:
`defect_evidence` when it is the category evidence for a planted defect; otherwise the verdict of
a reviewed ruling under `finding_reviews` in `scoreboard/adjudications.json` — `real` when it points
at a real problem (including a planted defect it was not keyword-matched to), `false_positive` when
it does not — or `unreviewed`. Precision is (defect evidence + real) / findings. While findings are
unreviewed it is shown as a range: unreviewed counted as false positives, then as real.

## How fixed variants are scored

A specimen may ship a `fixed.patch` that repairs every planted defect without other changes; its
`repro.sh` then reproduces none of them. The fixed copy is audited the same way (output in
`audit/fixed/`). Every finding on it is a false alarm unless a `finding_reviews` ruling marks it
`real` (a problem the patch leaves in place). "Still reported" counts planted defects whose
category evidence the audit keeps reporting after the fix.
